HOW IT WORKS

Round 1: Bid up to 15 virtual points. The highest bidder gets the chance to answer.

Correct: Bid amount + 5 points.

Wrong: Bid amount is deducted.

Round 2: Qualified teams use their virtual points to bid for medical equipment images.

Every Round 2 bid is deducted immediately from the team's current virtual points.

Round 2 result: Highest bid gets the answer chance. The host decides CORRECT or WRONG. Correct earns one device/image.

Final winner: Most devices/images won. If tied, highest remaining points wins.
